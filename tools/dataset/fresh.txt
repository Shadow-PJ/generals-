# Fresh orders for the final test of the order reader (session 3C), written by hand by Claude
# before the reader was built. Nothing trains on these, and nothing was tuned to them: they are
# only read by the eval, to see how the reader does on orders it has never met. Many of the
# groups are kinds of card that natural.txt doesn't have. Two orders that turned out to repeat
# natural.txt word for word were swapped for new ones during 3C.
#
# Same format and conventions as natural.txt:
#   - "== <order>" starts a group; the rule parser's card for that order is the expected card
#   - a step with no subject is done by everyone
#   - "him", "her", "it" and "them" after a condition mean the unit that set it off
#   - hurt and wounded = 50% HP, in trouble = 40%, low and weak = 30%; "group up" = 3 enemies
