## About this game

**Your army fights on its own. You give the orders.**

Generals is a 2D pixel-art strategy game where troops fight by themselves and you command. Before
each battle, type your orders in plain English ("If their Assassin dives my Ranger, protect her,
then everyone focus him") and your General turns each one into a Command card. During the
battle, fire your cards with five keys, at the moment that matters.

**Timing is the skill.** A card with a condition glows when its moment comes: fire it then for a
Perfect timing. Cards fired close together chain, cheaper and stronger; the right steps in a row
land signature combos. Fill your Momentum and fire your General's ultimate; fire it at the end
of a long chain for a Finisher.

**Every General plays like a different game.** You start with the Captain, who takes your orders
exactly as written. The five rulers you recruit each give every troop a skill, bring their own
ultimate, bend one rule about pips and read your orders their own way: the Warlord turns
retreats into counter-attacks, the Engineer digs in before moving, the Strategist suggests a
better moment.

**A campaign that changes every run.** Five regions, each ruled by a General with a boss rule of
their own. Every run is a path of battles, elite fights, events, a merchant and camps. Draft
fighters and boons, and build your army around faction bonuses. Wounds carry from fight to
fight, and losing a fight ends the run. Beat a ruler to recruit them and learn a Legendary order.

**Your command grows.** Command XP raises your rank, from simple one-step orders to orders with
conditions that fire again and again. Your company of eight veterans ranks up between runs.
Spend Insight on the Tech Web, equip artifacts, take Oaths of Command for harder runs and bigger
rewards, and earn each General's Mastery titles. After every battle, the Battle IQ report shows
your biggest mistake, best decision and the chance you missed.

**Battle a friend.** Versus matches over the internet: share a four-letter code, pick the map and
rank, write your orders in secret, then fight it out.

**Your words stay on your computer.** Orders are read by a rule parser and a small model trained
for this game that runs on your computer, offline. No account, no internet needed to play the
campaign.

## Features

- Write orders in plain English; your General turns them into Command cards (or build cards from menus)
- Real-time battles of 2 to 3 minutes, fought by five keys: four card slots and a Legendary slot, plus your ultimate
- Perfect timings, chains, signature combos and Finishers, recorded in a Combo Codex
- Six Generals with their own ultimates; the five you recruit also bring troop skills, a pip twist and their own way of reading orders
- Five troop classes (Vanguard, Ranger, Guardian, Invoker, Assassin), each with two specializations, and troop synergies
- A roguelite campaign across five regions with boss rulers, boons, duo boons, factions, artifacts and events
- Command Ranks, a company of veterans, a Tech Web, Mastery titles and Oaths of Command
- Battle IQ: a short report after every battle on what to do better
- Versus: battle a friend over the internet, cross-play between the Steam and browser builds
- Optional Tactical mode that pauses every 10 seconds
- Full controller and Steam Deck support, with an on-screen keyboard for orders
- Hand-made pixel art and synthesized music; plays offline

## Content notes

Fantasy combat between small pixel-art armies: troops fall with a puff of smoke, and hits show as
sparks and numbers; no blood or gore. No mature content. One optional download (an experimental
language model, off by default, about 400 MB) from Hugging Face; nothing else goes online except
Versus matches through the game's relay server.
